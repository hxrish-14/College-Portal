About Student Portal

A modern academic record system built for students and reviewers alike.

Project Overview

Student Portal lets a student sign in with their registration number and date of birth to view their profile, semester-wise results, SGPA, and CGPA — with a downloadable PDF copy of any semester and an offline mode that shows the last synchronized data when there's no connection.

Technology

Offline Cache

PDF Export
jsPDF

Security Note

Registration Number + Date of Birth is a convenience check, not strong authentication — a static site cannot keep a secret. See database.sql and README.md for the full explanation and safer alternatives.
